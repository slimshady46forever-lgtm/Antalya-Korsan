What to Consider

Beaches

  • Lara: Long sandy beach, Blue Flag, can be wavy
  • Konyaaltı: City beach, pebbly, calm water
  • Kemer: Small coves, clear water, mountains behind
  • Side: Long sandy beach
  • Alanya/Cleopatra: Golden sand

Family-friendly

For families with small children: Belek, Kundu, Side, Kızılağaç, Titreyengöl.

Nightlife

For younger travelers: Alanya, Kemer, Lara.

Nature & Quiet

Peace-seekers: Göynük, Beldibi, Belconti, Kızılağaç, Okurcalar.

History & Culture

Ancient ruins: Side, Kaleiçi, Kemer (Olympos, Phaselis).

Golf

Golf: Belek (15+ courses).

Day Trip Suggestions

  • Lara/Kundu → Side: 60 min, ancient ruins
  • Belek → Manavgat Waterfall: 35 min
  • Kemer → Olympos: 45 min, Yanartaş fire
  • Alanya → Dim River: 20 min, riverside restaurants

Conclusion
