Antalya Airport International Arrivals Guide (2026)

Transport Guide2026-02-055 min read

Antalya Airport's International Terminal (Terminal 1) is one of Turkey's most modern airports. After landing, you'll go through 4 main steps.

1. After Disembarking

You'll either be taken by bus to the terminal (remote stand) or walk in via a jet bridge. Usually takes 5–10 minutes.

Note: The terminal is large — about 500–700 metres of walking. Use the moving walkways if needed.

2. Passport Control

  • Off-peak: 10–20 min
  • Peak: 40–60 min
  • Summer season: 60+ min

Tip: E-gate lines are usually shorter.

3. Baggage Claim

  • Check the monitor for your belt number
  • Keep your baggage tag if luggage is lost
  • Long-strapped suitcases can jam — stand at the end of the belt

Average wait: 15–30 minutes.

4. Customs

  • Green Channel: Nothing to declare
  • Red Channel: High-value items, cash over €10,000

5. Arrivals Hall

After customs you enter the arrivals hall. Here you'll find:

  • Hotel and transfer signs
  • Free Wi-Fi (SMS verification)
  • Currency exchange and ATMs
  • Cafés and small shops
  • SIM card stands

Meeting Your VIP Driver

  1. Walk into the arrivals hall
  2. Look for drivers holding tall signs
  3. Find the sign with your name

Important: Share your flight code on WhatsApp in advance — for delays, your driver waits free of charge.

Things to Watch For

Unofficial "Taxi" Offers

Ignore anyone approaching you with "Need a taxi?". Use official taxi stands.

SIM Card Prices

Airport SIM cards cost more than in the city. For a short stay, roaming may be cheaper.

Currency Exchange

Airport rates are worse than in the city. Exchange only what you need for immediate expenses.

Total Time Estimate

StepDuration
Disembarking5–10 min
Passport control20–40 min
Baggage claim15–30 min
Customs2–5 min
Arrivals + driver5–10 min
Total50–95 min

From landing to your hotel: expect about 1.5–2.5 hours.
