How to Get from Antalya Airport to Your Hotel (2026 Guide)

Transport Guide2026-01-155 min read

Antalya Airport (AYT) is one of Turkey's busiest tourist airports, handling over 30 million passengers a year — most of them headed to resort areas. Once you land, you have 4 ways to reach your hotel. Your choice affects how the rest of your first day goes.

1. Havaş Bus

Havaş runs bus services from the airport to the city center and some resort areas. You'll find them right outside the arrivals exit.

  • Price: 100–250 ₺ per person
  • Pros: Cheapest option. Air-conditioned, on schedule.
  • Cons: Goes to specific stops, not to your hotel door. You may need a second transfer. Not practical for groups.
  • Best for: Solo travelers on a budget heading to the city center.

2. Taxi

There's always a taxi available at the arrivals exit. Antalya taxis run on meters, but long-distance fares are negotiable.

  • Price: 400–700 ₺ for Kundu/Lara, 1,200–1,800 ₺ for Side/Belek, 3,500–4,500 ₺ for Alanya
  • Pros: Available instantly. Door to door.
  • Cons: Foreign tourists may be quoted higher prices. Luggage space is limited. Cash required. No price guarantee in advance.
  • Best for: Short distances (Kundu, Lara) and single travelers.

3. VIP Transfer (Private Chauffeur)

Booked in advance, meets you with your name on a sign, drops you directly at your hotel.

  • Price: Sedan VIP from 480 ₺ (Kundu), around 4,000 ₺ for far destinations like Alanya
  • Pros: Price is fixed upfront — no surprises. Driver meets you by name. Luggage assistance included. Flight tracking. Air-conditioned, comfortable car. Door to door.
  • Cons: Requires advance booking (usually 2 hours ahead).
  • Best for: Families, groups, business travelers, first-time visitors, anyone who wants comfort.

4. Car Rental

Several rental agencies operate at the airport. Best for longer stays.

  • Price: From 800 ₺ per day + fuel + insurance
  • Pros: Total freedom.
  • Cons: You drive while tired. Parking hassles. You need to know local traffic.
  • Best for: Stays longer than a week, travelers who like to explore.

Steps After Landing

  1. Passport control (20–40 min, up to an hour at peak times)
  2. Baggage claim
  3. Customs
  4. Arrivals hall — your driver waits with a sign showing your name
