Antalya Transfer Tips for Families with Children

Family Guide2026-02-105 min read

Traveling with small children requires very different planning than adult-only trips. After a long flight, doing a transfer with tired kids can be tricky without proper preparation.

1. Ask About Child Seats in Advance

In Turkey, children under 4 must use a child seat or booster in vehicles. But not every transfer company stocks every seat type.

How it works with us:

  • Child seat request must be submitted in advance via WhatsApp
  • Availability and fee are confirmed during booking
  • Seat types: 0–13 kg infant, 9–18 kg child, 15–36 kg booster
  • Tell us your child's age and weight

Important: Don't assume the seat will be in the car. Don't travel without WhatsApp confirmation.

2. Luggage Planning

Families with small children carry more than average: stroller, formula, bottles, spare clothes, toys, medicine.

So sedan isn't enough:

PassengersRecommended Vehicle
2 adults + 1 babySedan
2 adults + 2 kidsMinivan
2 adults + 3+ kidsMinivan/Minibus
4 adults + 2 kidsMinibus

3. Choose the Right Flight Time

  • Early morning (06:00–09:00): Kids tired, sleep in the car
  • Evening (18:00–21:00): Easiest option
  • Night (22:00+): Can be tough
  • Midday (12:00–15:00): Kids active, may get bored

4. Keep Transfer Time Short

  • Choose hotels near the airport (Lara, Kundu)
  • For long distances, take a break (Manavgat, Serik)
  • Night transfers go faster — kids sleep

5. Keeping Kids Entertained

  • Tablet + headphones (downloaded cartoons)
  • Small toys
  • Snacks (dried fruit, crackers)
  • Water or juice (sippy bottle)
  • Stuffed toy / blanket

6. Motion Sickness

  • Light meal 30 min before the trip
  • You can ask the driver "please drive gently, kids in the car"
  • Keep a bag and wet wipes handy
  • Have kids look to the side, not forward
  • Motion sickness medicine if doctor-approved

7. Practical Airport Tips

  • Baby care rooms available
  • Stroller to cabin or gate-checked
  • Warm water from cafés for bottles
  • Changing tables in all restrooms

8. What to Share on WhatsApp

  • Number of children and their ages
  • Child seat request (how many, which age)
  • Stroller to be carried?
  • Special needs (allergies, medication)
  • Preferred AC temperature

Quick Summary

  • ✅ Ask about child seats in advance via WhatsApp
  • ✅ Choose minivan or minibus
  • ✅ Prefer evening flights
  • ✅ Pick short-distance areas
  • ✅ Pack tablet, snacks, water
  • ✅ Prepare for motion sickness
